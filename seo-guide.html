The Complete SEO Guide

Your Practical Guide to SEO.

A comprehensive collection of SEO checklists and tools to help you optimize pages, improve search visibility, strengthen your website, and track performance.

SECTION 01

On-Page SEO Checklist

Optimize page titles, content, headings, images, internal links, structured data, and user experience.

1

Head & Metadata

What Google indexes first

  • ✓ Title tag — 50–60 chars, primary keyword near the start.
  • ✓ Meta description — 150–160 chars, keyword + benefit + soft CTA.
  • ✓ Canonical URL set to prevent duplicates.
  • ✓ Open Graph — og:title, og:description, og:image (1200×630), og:url, og:type.
  • ✓ Twitter Card — summary_large_image, title, description, image.
  • ✓ Language attribute on <html> (e.g. lang="en").
  • ✓ Viewport meta tag for responsive rendering.
  • ✓ Favicon + Apple touch icon.
  • ✓ Charset meta — <meta charset="utf-8">.
2

URL Structure

Clean, readable, keyword-forward

  • ✓ Short slug — under 60 characters.
  • ✓ Primary keyword in the slug.
  • ✓ Hyphens only — never underscores.
  • ✓ Lowercase only.
  • ✓ No stop words (“the”, “a”, “of”) unless necessary.
  • ✓ Logical hierarchy — /services/slug/, /blog/slug/.
3

Headings

Structure for skimmers & bots

  • ✓ Exactly one H1 per page, contains primary keyword.
  • ✓ Logical H2 → H3 hierarchy — never skip levels.
  • ✓ H2s use supporting keywords + questions from the cluster.
  • ✓ No keyword stuffing — write naturally.
4

Copy & Body

Answer the query, fast

  • ✓Primary keyword in the first 100 words.
  • ✓Direct answer to the query in the first paragraph.
  • ✓Length matches SERP average (within 20% of top-3).
  • ✓Short paragraphs (1–4 sentences).
  • ✓Readability — 8th–10th grade level.
  • ✓Active voice preferred.
  • ✓Bold key phrases — sparingly.
  • ✓Bullets & numbered lists where appropriate.
5

FAQ Section

Every blog post

  • ✓4–8 questions from SEMrush Questions tab + “People Also Ask”.
  • ✓Direct answers — 2–4 sentences each.
  • ✓FAQ schema (JSON-LD) applied where appropriate.
6

Images

Every image is a ranking signal

  • ✓Alt text describes image + keyword where natural.
  • ✓Filenames — descriptive, hyphen-separated (e.g. emergency-plumber-toronto.webp).
  • ✓WebP, compressed under 200 KB where practical.
  • ✓Width/height attributes specified — prevents CLS.
  • ✓Lazy loading (loading="lazy") for below-fold images.
  • ✓Responsive srcset where needed.
  • ✓Featured/hero image for social sharing.
7

Internal Links

Pass authority across the site

  • ✓3–5 internal links per post.
  • ✓Link to related blog posts & relevant service pages.
  • ✓Descriptive anchor text — never “click here” or “read more”.
  • ✓Contextually placed in body copy.
  • ✓Breadcrumbs on every page.
8

External Links

Cite authority, don't hoard it

  • ✓2–3 external links to authoritative sources (.gov, .edu, major industry).
  • ✓Relevant to the topic.
  • ✓Open in new tab with rel="noopener" when appropriate.
  • ✓rel="nofollow" for sponsored links where required.
9

Schema Markup

JSON-LD in <head>

  • ✓Article schema on blog posts.
  • ✓LocalBusiness schema — most specific subtype (Plumber, Dentist...).
  • ✓Service schema on service pages.
  • ✓FAQ schema wherever FAQ section exists.
  • ✓BreadcrumbList schema on every page.
  • ✓Organization schema site-wide.
  • ✓Author/Person schema for bylines.
10

E-E-A-T Signals

Experience · Expertise · Authority · Trust

  • ✓Author byline with name on every blog post.
  • ✓Author bio with credentials (years, qualifications).
  • ✓Link to author's dedicated page.
  • ✓Published date displayed.
  • ✓"Last updated" date when refreshed.
  • ✓Real stories, numbers, opinions from the business voice file.
  • ✓Cite authoritative sources.
  • ✓About page with full company credentials.
  • ✓Contact page — real address, phone, hours.
11

Accessibility

A11y signals = SEO signals

  • ✓Semantic HTML5 — <header>, <nav>, <main>, <article>, <footer>.
  • ✓ARIA labels on interactive elements where needed.
  • ✓Color contrast meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 body text).
  • ✓Focus indicators visible on interactive elements.
  • ✓Alt text on all images (empty alt="" for decorative).
  • ✓Descriptive link text.
  • ✓Skip-to-content link for keyboard users.
12

Mobile & Responsive

Mobile-first indexing

  • ✓Responsive layout (Tailwind handles this).
  • ✓Touch targets minimum 48×48 px.
  • ✓Body font minimum 16 px.
  • ✓No horizontal scroll at any viewport.
  • ✓No intrusive interstitials.
13

Social Preview

Shareable card

  • ✓OG image optimized — 1200×630, under 1 MB.
  • ✓Twitter Card image — 1200×600.
  • ✓Compelling og:description — different from meta if valuable.
14

Conversion Elements

Capture the lead

  • ✓Primary CTA above the fold.
  • ✓Phone number with click-to-call (tel:).
  • ✓Multiple CTA placements throughout the page.
  • ✓Trust signals — reviews, ratings, licenses, years.
  • ✓Testimonials with names (photos where possible).
  • ✓Service-area coverage listed.
  • ✓Business hours displayed.
  • ✓Physical address with embedded map.
15

Long-Form Content

1500+ word posts

  • ✓Table of contents with anchor links at the top.
  • ✓Jump links for each H2.
  • ✓Back-to-top button.
16

Internal Linking

Link naturally, not by word count

  • ✓Blog posts: 3–6 links for 800–1,200 words.
  • ✓Collection pages: 2–5 relevant internal links.
  • ✓Product descriptions: 1–3 useful links.
  • ✓Prioritize relevance: link to related products, collections, and guides.
  • ✓Descriptive anchor text: explain what the destination offers.
  • ✓No forced ratios: every link should provide value.

SECTION 02

Technical SEO Checklist

Optimize your website's technical foundation with essential checks for crawling, indexing, site speed, Core Web Vitals, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonical URLs, redirects, mobile usability, and structured data. Ensure search engines can efficiently discover, understand, and index your content.

1

Page Speed

Improve loading performance

  • ✓Run PageSpeed Insights to identify performance bottlenecks.
  • ✓Optimize large images and reduce unnecessary scripts.
  • ✓Reduce render-blocking resources where practical.
  • ✓Review third-party scripts that slow down rendering or interactions.
  • ✓Re-test after changes to measure the impact.
  • ✓ Defer non-critical JavaScript: Use defer for external scripts that aren't needed immediately during HTML parsing. Test functionality and loading order after changes.
  • ✓Prioritize critical CSS: Consider inlining small, essential above-the-fold styles to reduce render-blocking requests.
2

Core Web Vitals

Measure the user experience

  • ✓LCP — Largest Contentful Paint: Is the largest visible element when loading the site, Aim for 2.5 seconds or less. Prioritize the main visible content; use eager loading and high fetch priority for the LCP image when appropriate.
  • ✓CLS — Cumulative Layout Shift: is when elements move after the page has loaded, mostly from a third party script, Aim for 0.1 or less. Reserve space for images, embeds, ads, and dynamically loaded content.
  • ✓INP — Interaction to Next Paint: Is the time that passed when the user interacted with an element speed index: Measures how quickly visible page content appears during loading. Aim for 200 ms or less. Reduce long JavaScript tasks and improve interaction responsiveness.
  • ✓FCP — First Contentful Paint: Is the first element loaded on the page, Monitor how quickly the first text or image appears.
  • ✓Speed Index: Monitor how quickly visible content fills in. Lower values are better; this is a diagnostic metric, not a Core Web Vital.
3

Crawlability & Indexability

Help Google discover your pages

  • ✓ Monitor Google Search Console: Review indexing reports, excluded pages, and crawl errors.
  • ✓ Inspect important URLs: Use URL Inspection to check indexing status and request indexing when appropriate.
  • ✓ Check robots.txt: Verify that /robots.txt doesn't unintentionally block important pages or resources.
  • ✓ Maintain your XML sitemap: Keep /sitemap.xml accessible, include preferred canonical URLs, and submit it in Search Console.
  • ✓ Set canonical URLs: Specify the preferred URL for duplicate or similar pages and align internal links accordingly.
  • ✓ Review noindex directives: Ensure pages intended for search results aren't accidentally marked noindex.
  • ✓ Fix broken or outdated URLs: Use a relevant 301 redirect when a suitable replacement exists; otherwise, return an appropriate 404 or 410 status.
4

Monitoring & Testing

Validate fixes and track results

  • ✓Google Search Console: Monitor indexing, crawl issues, and Core Web Vitals reports.
  • ✓URL Inspection: Test important pages and view Google's reported indexing information.
  • ✓PageSpeed Insights: Review lab diagnostics and available real-user performance data.
  • ✓Test mobile and desktop after significant theme or code changes.
  • ✓Recheck after fixes to confirm the issue is resolved.
  • ✓Request indexing: After meaningful page updates, use URL Inspection to request recrawling when appropriate. This doesn't guarantee immediate indexing.
  • ✓Monitor recrawling: Check Search Console for updated indexing status rather than assuming Google revisits every page on a fixed schedule.
5

JavaScript & Rendering

Make content accessible to crawlers

  • ✓Initial HTML: Google fetches and parses the HTML response to discover content and links.
  • ✓JavaScript rendering: Google may execute JavaScript later to access content and links generated by scripts.
  • ✓Server-side rendering (SSR): The server returns HTML containing the page content, making it available earlier to crawlers.
  • ✓Client-side rendering (CSR): JavaScript in the browser assembles or updates page content. Verify important content appears in Google's rendered HTML.
  • ✓Test rendered pages: Use Search Console URL Inspection to check rendered content and identify JavaScript errors or blocked resources.
6

HTTP Status Codes

Send the right response to crawlers

  • ✓200 — OK: The request succeeded. Ensure the page returns the intended content and status.
  • ✓301 — Permanent redirect: Redirect a moved URL to its relevant replacement and update internal links and sitemap entries.
  • ✓404 — Not found: Return a real 404 when content is missing and has no suitable replacement. Google may remove the URL from its index after processing the response.
  • ✓410 — Gone: Use when content has been permanently removed and you want to signal that explicitly.
  • ✓304 — Not modified: Allows a crawler to reuse a cached response when the resource hasn't changed, reducing data transfer.
  • ✓Check status codes: Audit important URLs for redirect loops, chains, unexpected 404s, and server errors.
7

Crawl Budget

Help Google crawl efficiently

  • ✓Crawl capacity: Google adjusts crawling based partly on server health, response times, and errors.
  • ✓Crawl demand: Influenced by URL popularity, content freshness, quality, and site inventory.
  • ✓Check Crawl Stats: In Google Search Console, review crawl requests, response codes, response times, and host status.
  • ✓Reduce URL waste: Consolidate duplicate URLs, fix redirect chains, and keep your sitemap current.
  • ✓Prioritize important pages: Ensure key pages are internally linked and discoverable.

SECTION 03

Off-Page SEO Checklist

Strengthen your website's authority and reputation beyond your own pages. Audit backlinks, earn links from relevant websites, build brand mentions, pursue digital PR, manage your online reputation, and monitor competitor link profiles. Focus on genuine relationships, relevant references, and trustworthy signals that support long-term search visibility.

1

Off-Page SEO Basics

Build authority beyond your website

  • ✓Understand off-page SEO: Activities outside your domain that influence visibility, reputation, and authority.
  • ✓Earn third-party citations: Get your brand, website, or expertise referenced by relevant websites.
  • ✓Prioritize relevance and trust: A meaningful mention from a respected industry source can be more valuable than many low-quality links.
  • ✓Think long term: Build genuine relationships and a reputation worth referencing.
2

Backlink Building

Earn relevant, quality links

  • ✓Audit existing backlinks: Review referring domains, link quality, and lost links.
  • ✓Find relevant opportunities: Identify industry blogs, publications, and trusted websites that cover your niche.
  • ✓Create link-worthy content: Publish original research, useful guides, resources, or expert insights.
  • ✓Avoid manipulative link schemes: Don't buy links intended to manipulate rankings or participate in link exchanges at scale.
3

Digital PR

Get featured by trusted sources

  • ✓Industry publications: Pitch useful stories, expert commentary, and relevant contributions.
  • ✓News articles: Share genuinely newsworthy announcements, research, or business milestones.
  • ✓Industry blogs: Build relationships with writers and publishers covering your niche.
  • ✓Track brand mentions: Follow up when relevant coverage mentions your business without linking to your website.
4

Awards & Recognition

Build credibility through recognition

  • ✓Find relevant awards: Research reputable industry awards and editorial roundups. Such as 50 best restaurants
  • ✓Check editorial standards: Prioritize credible selection criteria and genuine recognition.
  • ✓Evaluate paid placements: Sponsorships and paid features must not be presented as independent awards or endorsements.
  • ✓Review link attributes: Paid links should use appropriate rel="sponsored" or nofollow attributes.
5

Local SEO

Strengthen your local presence

  • ✓Optimize Google Business Profile: Keep business details, categories, hours, and website URL accurate.
  • ✓Add quality photos: Keep business images current and representative.
  • ✓Earn genuine reviews: Encourage honest customer feedback and respond professionally.
  • ✓Maintain consistent business details: Check your name, address, and phone number across relevant directories.
6

Community & Brand Mentions

Build a genuine online reputation

  • ✓Reddit and forums: Participate helpfully in relevant discussions without spamming links.
  • ✓Third-party recommendations: Monitor discussions where people independently recommend your business.
  • ✓Respond where appropriate: Correct inaccurate information and answer legitimate questions professionally.
  • ✓Monitor brand mentions: Track references across blogs, news sites, communities, and industry websites.
